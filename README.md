# Purveyors Broadside
### Economics App for D&amp;D 5e
This is a tool to help the Dungeon Master create complexity in their Campaigns. 

#### Community Size
1. ###### Thorp
   - 20-80 residents
1. ###### Hamlet
   - 81-400 residents
1. ###### Village
   - 401-900 residents
1. ###### Small Town
   - 901-2,000 residents
1. ###### Large Town
   - 2,001-5,000 residents
1. ###### Small City
   - 5,001-12,000 residents
1. ###### Large City
   - 12,001-25,000 residents
1. ###### Metropolis
   - 25,001 or more residents
